# The Pine Cache

**Player-facing recovered correspondence | Dr. Amelia Pine's terminal, Starhaven Biolabs**
*GM note: this is the cleaned, canon-current rewrite of the email lore-dump players access on Pine's machine. Threads are ordered as a curious player would unspool them — most recent first on the terminal, but presented here chronologically for GM legibility. Redactions (████) are in the source files and are deliberate: they mark where Pine or her correspondents trimmed names the players aren't meant to have yet. Everything here is diegetic; nothing names the Lorekeepers, and the cosmological material stays in-world-incomplete.*

---

## In-Universe Conventions (GM reference)

**Address domains** (the Federation's network naming — worth knowing so the addresses *read* as clues):
- `.fed` — Federation government and institutional
- `.lib` — Libra regional (Starhaven, Libra University, the Coast administration)
- `.sec` — secured/encrypted routing (Scepter and other off-grid traffic; a `.sec` address is itself a small tell)
- `.com` equivalent is `.net` in-setting — ordinary commercial mail

**Names anchored to current canon:**
- **Starhaven Biolabs** = the medical-research institute of Libra University; Pine's cover posting and the players' accessible site. *Not* the weapons facility.
- **Biolabs Site Alpha** = the restricted facility where Project Chimera (hybridization science) and the Veerulent weaponization pipeline actually run. Pine coordinates with it at a distance — her Starhaven address is the quiet door.
- **Director Antonio Bellini** = Site Alpha's head (canon).
- **Dr. Ramon Galceran** = Pine's Project Proteus colleague at Site Alpha (the P-1 researcher). *(Renamed from a placeholder; Libran register.)*
- **MB** = Pine's Scepter handler; high-ranking, unidentified in the cache.
- **Professor Yucca** = the players' starting professor at Libra University. *(Note: "Yucca" is a family name and bears no relation to Rayn Yucca the Unifier — no collision, kept as canon.)*
- The party as the cache references them: **Joey, Hiro, Poltymus, Rixso** (adjust to current roster; Kameo and others post-date the cache and shouldn't appear).

## THREAD 6 — Pine & "S.R." (shadow research)

> **From:** s.r.@████.sec
> **To:** a.pine@starhaven.lib
> **Subject:** The door-work
>
> Dr. Pine,
>
> Progress on the corruption project is real but slow, and slow is the only safe speed. The influence does not "transfer" so much as it is *invited* — the subject has to be opened, and the opening does not close on its own afterward. We are learning the closing. It is harder than the opening by an order I did not expect.
>
> The Twilight practitioners up north know things we don't, and won't share them. I'd give a great deal for one of their people on staff. Failing that, I'll keep paying for what washes down from the Depths.
>
> One more thing, and I want it on a record only you and I read: the phenomenon is not ours alone. I've made contact with a group operating out of **Orre** — yes, *that* far, the other side of the world, where nobody we answer to is looking. They've been engineering corrupted Pokémon for years by their own method, a "closing of the heart's door." Their results are cruder than what the Depths produce here, but they have *volume*, and they have a man named **Ein** who has written down more about the process than anyone on our side dares to. The distance is the whole appeal — what we trade with Orre never touches a Federation wire. I think the same darkness surfaces wherever the world wears thin. They just found a different tap on it.
> S.R.

> **From:** a.pine@starhaven.lib
> **To:** s.r.@████.sec
> **Subject:** Re: The door-work
>
> Understood, and agreed on the pace. MB wants results; I keep telling MB that this is not a thing one hurries without making a monster nobody commands. The orb work and the door-work should not touch — keep your subjects far from anything resonant until we know what proximity does.
>
> The Orre channel is sanctioned — quietly. MB likes that it's deniable and offshore; a courier line that crosses an ocean is a courier line no one here can subpoena. Take what Ein's people have learned and improve on it; do not let them learn what *we* know in return. They think the corruption is a technique. We know better. Keep that asymmetry.
>
> "E" will be passing through your area. Give him what he needs and keep him on a short tether. You know what he is.
> A.

> **From:** s.r.@████.sec
> **To:** a.pine@starhaven.lib
> **Subject:** Re: The door-work
>
> "E" is the last thing my project needs near it. But I know better than to refuse. I'll keep the corridor clear and the cameras off.
> S.R.

*(GM: shadow corruption's primary canon is native — the Whispering Shadow's influence by way of the Depths of Twilight, with the Driftlands' Twilight Watchers ("the practitioners up north") holding the acute-exposure purification knowledge S.R. covets and can't buy. The **Orre/Cipher** connection sits on top of that, reconciled rather than imported: Shima is a continent very far from the rest of the Pokémon world, which still exists but rarely touches Shiman affairs. **Cipher** — operating an ocean away in Orre under the scientist **Ein** — engineers Shadow Pokémon by their own "closed-heart" method, and S.R.'s reading is the canon-correct one: the same darkness surfaces wherever the world wears thin, and Orre is simply a different tap on it. The distance is a feature — a deniable, un-subpoenable offshore channel for technique-sharing — and the asymmetry Pine insists on (Cipher thinks it's a technique; Scepter knows it's cosmological) keeps the foreign thread from ever overshadowing the native one. **Pull-weight: deliberately light.** Orre is a flavor of reach and a source of crude technique, not a plot the party must chase across the world; treat it as proof that Scepter's arms are long, not as a passport. "E" is **Emmanuel Disco** — Cipher's flamboyant "special consultant" (a Miror B.–type), the one Orre import who actually shows up in-theater; he travels with the enforcer **Ignas**, who does his violence for him. ## GM Notes — Using the Cache

**What the cache proves, in ascending order of player payoff:**
1. The friendly starting professor was unknowingly feeding a Scepter operative information about the party (Thread 1) — the first gut-punch.
2. Pine is Scepter, embedded in a respectable institute (Threads 2, 4, 5).
3. Scepter is *using the party as living keys* to open ruins it can't enter — the orb-hunt is deliberate, and the party are unwitting instruments (Threads 2, 7).
4. The Aridune refugee crisis was a deliberate Federation weapons deployment (the Veerulent program), which Scepter exploits for recruitment (Thread 5) — the campaign's central political wound, in writing.
5. Shadow corruption is real, studied, and dangerous to handle (Thread 6).
6. Scepter is assembling *multiple* primordial artifacts — the orb is one of a set (Thread 7).
7. Something none of the conspirators controls is already moving, and a weapon-specimen is loose (Thread 8) — the hook that walks out of the terminal and into the campaign.

**Redaction discipline:** keep MB and the `.sec` addresses redacted. The cache should raise *more* questions than it answers — the players should leave Pine's terminal knowing they've found something huge and not yet knowing how huge.

**Threads you can pull later:** P-1's escape and what it left at the breach (Thread 4); the loose specimen at Site Alpha (Thread 8); Maxine Blaze as recruit-or-redeem (Thread 5); S.R.'s shadow-corruption subjects, their Twilight-Watcher connection, and the deniable Orre/Cipher channel (Thread 6); the second artifact, the spear (Thread 7); and Pine herself, alive, pseudonymous, and authoring revolution propaganda somewhere downstream.

**Canon guardrails honored:** Chimera/Veerulent at Site Alpha not Starhaven; Bellini as Site Alpha director; the Orre/Cipher thread reconciled as a deniable offshore channel atop the native shadow cosmology (Cipher's distance is the point; it never overshadows the Depths-of-Twilight canon); the Lorekeepers never named; the primordial artifacts referenced but not named; the Veerulent program as the canonical bioweapon pipeline with the Federation as originator and Scepter as exploiter.
